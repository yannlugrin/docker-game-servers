**2. Minor — Rule 2, pinned-tools bullet: "covered like anything else shipped" is undefined for binaries.** "The shipped operator tools enter pinned, their version or digest recorded, and are covered like anything else shipped." For the static query/RCON/SQLite clients — downloaded binaries, not source the linters reach — the check families of rule 2 have nothing that applies, so "covered" invites either a minimal reading (the pin and the recorded digest *are* the coverage) or a maximal one (invent verification machinery), and rule 11 pulls one way while "covered like anything else" pulls the other. Direction: say explicitly that for shipped third-party binaries the pin plus recorded version/digest satisfies the coverage obligation.

**3. Minor — Rule 1's open-facts classifier under-decides pre-committed paths that add a port-table row.** The classifier's "documented operator surface" is glossed "(a variable dropped or added)", and the always-comes-back examples are d, e, f, g, k, l. The wrong guess is safe (a spare round-trip, or one autonomous amendment the operator sees in the step summary), so this is minor — but the classifier could name whether a documented port/listener addition counts as surface.
